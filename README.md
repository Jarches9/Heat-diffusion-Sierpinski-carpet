# Heat Diffusion on a Sierpinski Carpet
Discrete visualization of heat diffusion on a fractal surface known as the Sierpinski Carpet

## Overview

The simulation compares:

- A Sierpinski carpet after two iterations
- A Sierpinski carpet after three iterations
- A Regular square used as a control

## Methods

The heat equation is discretized numerically in Python.

The main quantities analyzed are:
- Maximum temperature over time
- Normalized spatial spread over time

  ## Results

  The Sierpinski carpet maintains a higher maximum temperature and shows a slower spatial spreading than the regular square.

  ## Technologies

  - Python
  - NumPy
  - Matplotlib
  - Jupyter Notebook
